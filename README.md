# Wines Export – Ufficio Export (remote MCP server)

The export office of Wines Export inside your AI chat: buyers, shipments, documents.

Wines Export is a Rome-based agency that works only with Italian wineries. It does what a winery needs to get found and to sell, in Italy and abroad: the website, social media content, visit and tasting bookings, the online shop, the Google listing and reviews, technical sheets and price lists in several languages, foreign buyers and trade-fair preparation.

This repository documents the Wines Export remote MCP server, listed in the official MCP Registry as `com.wines-export/ufficio-export`. It contains no server code.

## What you can ask
- Foreign buyers of Italian wine, country by country
- The documents and requirements for exporting wine
- The winery's export shipments

## Connect
- Endpoint: `https://mcp.wines-export.com/mcp` (Streamable HTTP)
- Authentication: OAuth. You sign in with your Wines Export access link or your CRM credentials.
- Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name: Wines Export. URL: the endpoint above.
- ChatGPT: Settings → Apps and connectors → Developer mode. New connector named Wines Export, OAuth authentication, URL: the endpoint above.
- Step-by-step guide: https://www.wines-export.com/connetti

## For wineries and buyers
- Free check for Italian wineries: https://www.wines-export.com/#controllo
- Importers and buyers who want to be introduced to Italian producers: https://www.wines-export.com/controllo?l=en&chi=compratore
- Wines Export Observatory, every month: which Italian denominations buyers carry, by country: https://www.wines-export.com/en/observatory
- Italian wine buyers by country: https://www.wines-export.com/en/wine-importers

Wines Export does not sell wine, does not ship it and does not handle customs: transport and customs stay with the winery's partners.
